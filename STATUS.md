# Gebrauchtwaren-Buch: Differenzbesteuerung (§25a UStG) für Reseller auf eBay, Vinted, Kleinanzeigen …

**Stage:** scouted · **Platform:** web · **Score:** 3.3/5 · **Spend:** $0.00

For VAT-registered German resellers who buy used goods from private persons: a browser tool that keeps the per-item purchase and sale record §25a requires, writes the purchase receipt (Ankaufbeleg) for a private seller, works out the margin VAT per item or by the Gesamtdifferenz method, and outputs the monthly VAT-return figures and a DATEV booking file. Unlike the only dedicated tool (Vintax), it is not tied to Shopify.

**Why now:** standing need. Window: Standing gap: lexoffice and sevdesk users have said for over a year that their tools cannot handle §25a. The first dedicated tool (Vintax) launched on 2026-10-15 but only inside Shopify. It or others could add eBay/Vinted imports, so a non-Shopify version has months, not years, of head start.

## Alternatives found

- Vintax: Margin Scheme VAT (Shopify app) (solid): Works only inside a Shopify store. No import from eBay, Vinted or Kleinanzeigen, and nothing for flea-market or shop purchases. 19 to 79 USD a month. Launched 2026-10-15, no reviews yet.
- Xentral Differenzbesteuerung module (partial): A module of a full ERP, oversized for a one- or two-person reseller (price not checked).
- lexoffice / sevdesk (partial): Users in the thread say they cannot handle §25a margins properly. sevdesk offers only a free invoice template.
- Export für DATEV (Shopify app) (partial): Its developer says it builds the monthly DATEV batch but does not calculate margin taxation.
- deutschland-rechner Differenzbesteuerung-Rechner (partial): A one-off calculator for a single item: no record book, no receipts, no monthly totals (seen in search results, not opened).
- JTL-Wawi (partial): Free desktop ERP for online sellers. I could not verify whether it supports §25a, and it is heavy Windows software for a small reseller.
